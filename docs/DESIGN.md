# The Jacaranda Edit

## Composition locked before modeling

- A three-quarter, slightly elevated orthographic view of an imagined Sydney corner. The building sits a little left of the diorama's centre; its front and curved right façade remain visible.
- A cream three-storey heritage building is the focal point. Two continuous wraparound balconies and the rounded cornice repeat the same curve. Arched French windows and fine dark ironwork give scale.
- A high, spreading tree behind the left elevation; an independently shaped taller canopy behind the right; a smaller, leaning tree in the left foreground; and a slender, lower tree near the right-hand lantern. The canopies are separated sprays of flowers on real branching structures, with open sky between them.
- Keep the front door, corner balcony curves and purple bench unobstructed. Let flowers frame these details instead of covering them.
- Sandstone pavement and a short curved street sit on a shallow, rounded miniature plinth. A book and a cup on the bench, restrained planting and a handful of petals make the place inhabited without adding people.

## Palette and rendering

Paper #f7f5ef; ink #403549; cream #eee2cc; lavender #aa87ce; deep blossom #80619f; iron #55474e; pavement #d7d0c2; warm light #ffd59a.

Soft illustrated 3D: low-contrast diffuse shading, rounded mouldings, matte plaster, fine faceted petals, soft contact shadows, no heavy cartoon outlines. Default is late-afternoon lavender hour. Night uses blue-plum ambient light and local amber pools from lanterns, visible through real gaps in the branches.
